Simple Ways to Save Electricity

1. Keep AC at 24°C Instead of 21°C in Quiet Rooms

Floor 3 has fewer people. Setting the air conditioner temperature slightly warmer (around 24°C) keeps the room comfortable and saves a large amount of electricity.

2. Use Window Sunlight Instead of Lights During Day

Between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM, bright daylight comes in from big windows. Dimming or turning off the lights right next to windows saves easy electricity.

3. Don't Turn on Big AC Chillers at the Same Minute

When turning on large cooling chillers in the morning, wait 15 minutes between each machine. Starting both at once causes a big power surge that drives up electricity bills.

4. Keep Server Room Doors Closed Tight

Server Room 203 gets warm easily. Keeping the doors closed keeps cold air inside the room, so the cooling fans do not have to run at maximum speed all day.

5. Cool the Building Early in the Morning

Turn on AC slightly earlier around 6:00 AM when electricity is cheap and outside air is cool. By the hot afternoon (1:00 PM – 4:00 PM), the building is already cool, so the AC can rest.
